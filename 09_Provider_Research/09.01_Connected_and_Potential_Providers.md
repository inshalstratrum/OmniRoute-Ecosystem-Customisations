## Validation for each newly connected provider
Check official signup method, card requirement, recurring vs one-time credits, auto-billing hard stop, commercial/ToS privacy terms, exact OpenAI compatibility, token-limit and tools/vision, current free models. Never let an unverified candidate enter production fallback automatically.
