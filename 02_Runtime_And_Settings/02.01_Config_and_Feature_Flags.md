## Warning about applicability
- Free/strict settings may apply to certain auto-routing paths but not every direct paid model ID, saved Combo or externally configured provider billing policy.
- The special route auto/coding:pro means premium-tier metadata, NOT 'scarce one-time free reserve'.
- A process started against the incorrect DATA_DIR may read different settings. Confirm active data path and loopback listener.
- Do not conflate persisted policy with end-to-end tests.
